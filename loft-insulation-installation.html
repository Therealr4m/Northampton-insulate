A LITTLE MORE DETAIL

What to consider before installing loft insulation

An effective installation starts with understanding the existing loft, rather than choosing a product in isolation.

↗

Existing insulation

Check whether any old insulation is present, uneven or damaged before deciding what should stay or be removed.

↗

Coverage and access

Joists, hard-to-reach edges, hatches and services can affect how insulation is installed.

↗

Ventilation and fittings

The installation approach must account for roof-space ventilation and items such as downlights, cables or pipework.

↗

Storage plans

If you also want boarding, discuss this at the same time so the insulation is not squashed under boards.

Every property is different. Get in touch to discuss the circumstances of your loft before deciding what work is suitable.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Good to know.
Before you begin.

Looking for something more specific? We're happy to discuss your loft and the services available.

Ask us a question ↗
What does a full loft insulation installation involve?+

The work depends on the layout and condition of the loft, including access, existing insulation and ventilation. Contact us to discuss your property.

Will loft insulation help keep my home warmer?+

Loft insulation can reduce heat loss through the roof. The outcome varies with the building and its existing insulation.

Can I still use my loft for storage?+

Storage requirements should be considered before installation. You can also ask us about loft boarding.

Is every loft suitable for the same insulation?+

No. Loft construction, existing material, access and ventilation influence the suitable approach.

Should I remove old insulation first?+

Not always. Existing insulation may be suitable to retain, top up, or remove depending on its condition and the work required.
