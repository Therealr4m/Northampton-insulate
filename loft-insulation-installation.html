NEW LOFT INSULATION

Make more of the warmth already in your home.

Loft insulation is one of the ways to help limit heat escaping through a roof. The right approach depends on the layout and condition of the loft.

When to consider installation

A loft with little or no insulation may be losing heat through the roof. Before choosing an approach, consider the size and shape of the loft and whether it is dry and accessible.

What needs checking

Existing insulation, roof ventilation, pipes, cables and recessed lighting can all influence how insulation should be installed. The appropriate material and depth depend on the property.

After the work

Avoid covering ventilation openings or compressing insulation under stored items. If you want boarding as well, ask about a suitable raised system.

The best approach depends on your property's condition. These notes are general guidance, not a property survey.

Points to consider

  • Current insulation and any gaps
  • Ventilation at the eaves
  • Loft access and safe working space
