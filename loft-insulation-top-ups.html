ADDING INSULATION WHERE NEEDED

Sometimes a little extra makes a difference.

Existing loft insulation can settle, be moved or fall short of current recommendations. Whether a top-up is suitable depends on its condition and the space.

Is a top-up worthwhile?

Existing insulation can be uneven, moved aside or thinner than current guidance suggests. First, check whether the material is clean, dry and suitable to retain.

Where the gaps can appear

Loft hatches, areas around services and hard-to-reach edges may be overlooked. It is important not to obstruct ventilation or create unsafe contact with electrical fittings.

A considered improvement

If the original insulation is sound, topping up may be appropriate. Any new material should be compatible with what is already in place.

The best approach depends on your property's condition. These notes are general guidance, not a property survey.

Points to consider

  • Condition and depth of existing insulation
  • Gaps and difficult-to-reach areas
  • Ventilation and electrical clearances
