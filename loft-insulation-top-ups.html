loft insulation can settle, be moved or fall short of current recommendations. Whether a top-up is suitable depends on its condition and the space.

Is a top-up worthwhile?

If your existing insulation is in good condition but lacks sufficient depth, adding another layer can improve heat retention without needing a full replacement.

Where the gaps can appear

Heat can escape through poorly insulated areas around loft hatches, pipework and hard-to-reach edges. These areas need careful attention while keeping ventilation and electrical fittings clear.

Points to consider

  • Condition and depth of existing insulation
  • Gaps and difficult-to-reach areas
  • Ventilation and electrical clearances
