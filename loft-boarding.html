A LITTLE MORE DETAIL

Making storage and insulation work together

Useful loft storage needs thoughtful planning, including what the floor structure can carry and how insulation is protected.

↗

Suitable storage use

Loft boarding is generally considered for suitable light storage, not as a conversion to habitable living space.

↗

Structure and loading

The suitability of joists and boarding supports must be assessed for intended loads.

↗

Insulation below boards

Existing or new insulation should not be unnecessarily compressed by the boarding system.

↗

Access and airflow

Hatch access, safe movement and roof ventilation remain important alongside extra storage space.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Good to know.
Before you begin.

Looking for something more specific? We're happy to discuss your loft and the services available.

Ask us a question ↗
Can loft boarding be fitted over insulation?+

The design needs to avoid compressing insulation and must account for the structure and ventilation. The right solution depends on the loft.

Does loft boarding make my loft a living room?+

No. Boarding alone does not make a loft habitable or suitable for unrestricted loading.

Can you discuss insulation and boarding together?+

Yes. Tell us if you are considering both services so the options can be considered together.

Can I store anything I want in a boarded loft?+

No. Suitability depends on the structure and safe load capacity; boarding does not turn a loft into a fully rated floor.

Will boarding compress my insulation?+

A suitable raised boarding arrangement may help protect insulation depth, but the design must be considered for the individual loft.
