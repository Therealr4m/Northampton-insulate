COMMON QUESTIONS

Good to know.
Before you begin.

Looking for something more specific? We're happy to discuss your loft and the services available.

Ask us a question ↗
Why might loft insulation need removing?+

Reasons can include damage, planned refurbishment or changes to the loft. The need for removal should be assessed for each property.

Can any insulation be removed?+

Some materials require specialist assessment or handling. If you suspect asbestos or another hazardous material, do not disturb it; seek qualified advice.

Can insulation be replaced afterwards?+

Replacement may be an option depending on your loft. Ask about full loft insulation installation or top-ups.

Can all old loft insulation be treated the same way?+

No. Materials and their condition vary. Suspected hazardous materials should be identified and dealt with by appropriately qualified specialists.

Will removal automatically make my home warmer?+

Removal on its own does not improve thermal performance; it may be part of preparing for appropriate replacement insulation.
