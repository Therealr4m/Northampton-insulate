Reduce Heat Loss and Improve Home Comfort

When your roof is poorly insulated, heat from your living spaces can escape upwards. Your heating system then has to work harder to maintain a comfortable temperature, potentially increasing the amount of energy your household uses.

Installing suitable loft insulation helps slow this heat transfer. For Overstone homeowners, the benefits can include:

  • Less heat escaping through the roof and ceilings.
  • Better heat retention during colder months.
  • Potential reductions in household heating costs.
  • Improved overall thermal efficiency.
  • A more comfortable indoor environment.
  • Better use of the energy you already pay for.

The savings will depend on the property's starting condition, heating habits and energy consumption. However, addressing inadequate loft insulation is a practical way to reduce avoidable heat loss.

We also pay attention to areas that can easily be overlooked, such as the loft hatch, accessible edges and gaps around pipework. Proper coverage and suitable clearances matter just as much as the insulation material itself.
