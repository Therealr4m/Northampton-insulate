Getting more from insulation already in place

Existing depth and gaps

Condition of current insulation

Ventilation and services

COMMON QUESTIONS

Good to know.
Before you begin.

Looking for something more specific? We're happy to discuss your loft and the services available.

Ask us a question ↗
How do I know if my loft needs a top-up?+

The amount, coverage and condition of your existing insulation are important. A property-specific assessment is the best way to decide.

Can you add insulation on top of what is there?+

Sometimes. Existing material should be suitable and any moisture or ventilation concerns addressed first.

Will a top-up eliminate all draughts?+

Not necessarily. Draughts can come from several areas of a home and may need separate attention.

Is a top-up always cheaper than a new installation?+

Costs and suitability depend on access, existing insulation and what work is required; we have not quoted a fixed price.

Can insulation just be added over wet material?+

Wet or damaged material and the source of moisture should be assessed before any additional insulation is considered.
