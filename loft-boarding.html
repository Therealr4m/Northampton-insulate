LOFT ACCESS AND STORAGE

A little more room to make life easier.

Boarding needs to account for the structure of the loft and existing insulation. Suitable access, ventilation and load capacity all matter.

Making better use of the loft

Boarding can make suitable areas of a loft easier to reach and use for light storage, but not every structure is designed for extra loads.

Protecting the insulation

Directly laying boards onto thick insulation can compress it. A suitable approach may involve raised supports that preserve insulation and airflow.

Access and suitability

Safe access, the loft hatch, ceiling structure, services and ventilation should all be considered before boarding.

The best approach depends on your property's condition. These notes are general guidance, not a property survey.

Points to consider

  • Existing insulation depth
  • Appropriate structural load capacity
  • Access and safe routes through the loft
